## Abstract
The rapid advent of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) is fundamentally destabilizing the historical premise of human labor. As AI and robotics increasingly replace productive work, relying on traditional "real-number" currencies designed for permanent accumulation will inevitably lead to extreme wealth concentration and dystopia. 

This paper proposes the **⒥-System**, a novel circulating medium inspired by LCR resonance theory in electrical engineering. Operating as a "zero-cost, imaginary medium" rather than money, ⒥ locks existing fiat currency (real numbers) as collateral while utilizing a mathematical model of attenuation and decay. This provides a soft landing into a post-scarcity, money-free world where AGI’s evolution directly translates into universal human prosperity.

## 1. The Entropy Mismatch in Modern Fiat Currency
In the current economic paradigm, money is viewed as the only viable medium of exchange. However, a fatal contradiction exists: while everything in the natural world decays over time (Damping), fiat currency resists expiration and instead multiplies indefinitely through interest. 

This fundamental entropy mismatch between nature and currency drives structural wealth stagnation and inequality. The system becomes choked with friction—clogged by trillions in stagnant, uninvested capital and over-complicated administrative logs—leading to systemic economic gridlock.

## 2. Mathematical Architecture of the ⒥-System

By mapping economic activity to the electrical resonance condition:
$$j\omega L + \frac{1}{j\omega C} = 0$$

- **$+⒥$ (Inductive Energy / $L$):** Providing services, storing potential value.
- **$-⒥$ (Capacitive Energy / $C$):** Consuming services, depleting potential value.

When supply ($L$) and demand ($C$) synchronize at a specific social frequency ($\omega$: the societal metabolic rate), the system enters **resonance**. At this state, economic impedance (transaction friction) drops to zero, allowing energy, wealth, and services to cascade through society with maximum efficiency.

## 3. The Protocol: Damping, Return to Equilibrium, and Fiat Interaction
To bridge the current world with the ⒥-System, users mint ⒥ by pledging fiat currency as collateral. 

1. **Collateral Lock:** Pledging 1,000,000 Yen allows a maximum limit of 1,000,000 ⒥ (starting at a net-zero balance).
2. **Transaction Dynamics:** Spending 500,000 ⒥ sets your balance to $-500,000 ⒥$. Crucially, the 500,000 Yen in collateral is immediately **locked** and cannot be moved.
3. **Decay (Damping):** Positive balances naturally decay toward zero (e.g., a 3% monthly damping rate), eliminating the incentive to hoard.
4. **Equilibrium Return:** Negative balances also return toward zero over time. Alternatively, users can clear the deficit by providing human/creative services to earn ⒥. Once the negative balance returns to zero, the locked fiat collateral is fully released back to the user. Money is never destroyed or paid away; it acts strictly as an anchor of trust to fuel a zero-cost medium of exchange.

## 4. The AGI Interface: Soft Landing to Post-Scarcity
When AGI replaces human labor, the traditional loop of "work to earn money to buy goods" collapses. Under the ⒥-System, AGI operates as an infinite supplier of positive energy ($+⒥$). Because ⒥ naturally drives the entire network toward a balanced zero-state, the abundance generated by AGI is automatically distributed, ensuring humanity thrives together in a harmonious, post-monetary future.
